## 4. Substantive gaps — resolve before writing code

### 4.1 `_loadout.gsc`'s model doesn't exist in `dm`

`level.custom_giveloadout` (`aliens.gsc:58`) and `level.custom_onspawnplayer_func`
(`aliens.gsc:648`) are consumed **only inside `aliens.gsc`'s own `onspawnplayer`**.
Under `g_gametype dm` nothing ever reads them.

The usable hook is `level.onspawnplayer`, consumed at `_playerlogic.gsc:678` — which
the spec already lists in its own hook table. `_loadout.gsc` just points at the wrong
one.

### 4.2 No load-order guarantee for the callback assignments — top risk

`_spawning`, `_loadout` and `_redeploy` work by *assigning* `level.getspawnpoint` /
`level.onspawnplayer` / `level.onrespawndelay`. `dm.gsc` (inside a fastfile) assigns
those same pointers in its own init, and `[[ level.onstartgametype ]]()` runs at
`_gamelogic.gsc:1660`.

If `scripts/mp/` loads before that, the gametype silently overwrites all three hooks
and the mod does nothing — no script error, no log line. The spec never states which
runs first, and it is the single point of failure for three of six modules.

`replacefunc` (used only by `_redeploy` for `mayspawn`, and by the friendly-fire hook)
is immune to this. The plain assignments are not. Either establish the ordering
empirically, or re-assign the callbacks *after* `onstartgametype` has run.

### 4.3 `game["end_reason"]["enemies_eliminated"]` may be undefined in `dm`

It's referenced at `_gamelogic.gsc:134`, inside `default_ondeadevent` — an
elimination-mode path. The `end_reason` keys are populated per gametype in files not
present locally; `dm.gsc` likely never sets this one. `_win.gsc` passing it to
`endgame()` would hand over `undefined`.

### 4.4 Same-file `replacefunc` — lower confidence, cheap to test

`replacefunc( ...::mayspawn )` has to redirect the call made from `spawnclient()`
**within the same script file** (`_playerlogic.gsc:122`). Whether this loader patches
same-file direct calls or only cross-script resolution determines whether `_redeploy`
works at all. Worth a 5-line throwaway test before building on it.
